Plagiarism Awareness

Plagiarism is presenting another person's words, ideas, research, or creative work as your own without proper acknowledgment. It is a violation of academic integrity and can damage a student's credibility.

Common Types of Plagiarism

  • Direct plagiarism: Copying another person's exact words without quotation marks or citation.
  • Self-plagiarism: Reusing your previous work without permission when it is prohibited.
  • Mosaic plagiarism: Combining copied phrases or ideas from different sources without proper acknowledgment.
  • Accidental plagiarism: Failing to cite a source because of incorrect or missing references.
  • Paraphrasing plagiarism: Changing a few words but presenting another person's ideas as your own.

Why Is Plagiarism Wrong?

It takes credit away from original creators, misrepresents a student's knowledge, and reduces trust in academic work.

Possible Consequences

  • Receiving a failing grade or academic penalty.
  • Being required to redo an assignment.
  • Disciplinary action based on school policies.
  • Loss of trust and academic credibility.

How to Avoid Plagiarism

  • Write using your own understanding.
  • Use quotation marks for exact words.
  • Cite every borrowed idea or information.
  • Keep a record of your research sources.
  • Learn how to paraphrase correctly.
  • Review your references before submission.
Example: Instead of copying a paragraph from an article, understand the idea, explain it in your own words, and cite the original source.

Remember

Academic honesty means giving credit where credit is due and showing your own effort in every academic activity.

Citation & Referencing

Citation is the process of acknowledging the original source of information used in academic work. A reference list provides the details readers need to locate those sources.

Why Is Citation Important?

  • Gives credit to original authors.
  • Helps prevent plagiarism.
  • Supports arguments with reliable evidence.
  • Allows readers to verify information.
  • Shows that research was conducted.

APA 7th Edition

APA is a commonly used academic citation style. It includes author-date in-text citations and a reference list.

Book reference format:
Author, A. A. (Year). Title of book. Publisher.
Website reference format:
Author, A. A. (Year, Month Day). Title of webpage. Website Name. URL

In-text Citation Examples

Parenthetical: (Santos, 2024)
Narrative: Santos (2024) explained that responsible technology use is important.

Direct Quotes and Paraphrasing

  • Direct quote: Uses the exact words of the author, with quotation marks and an appropriate citation.
  • Paraphrase: Explains an author's idea in your own words while still giving credit.

Good Citation Practices

  • Check the spelling of author names.
  • Use the correct publication date.
  • Include the required source details.
  • Make sure every in-text citation has a matching reference entry.
  • Follow your instructor's required format.

Copyright & Fair Use

Copyright is a legal protection for original creative works. It gives creators certain rights over the reproduction, distribution, and use of their work, subject to applicable laws and limitations.

Examples of Copyrighted Works

  • Books, articles, and written materials.
  • Photographs, drawings, and illustrations.
  • Music, sound recordings, and videos.
  • Movies and other audiovisual works.
  • Computer programs and software.
  • Original digital artwork and designs.

What Is Fair Use?

Fair use is a legal limitation or exception that may allow certain uses of copyrighted material without permission. Whether a use qualifies depends on the applicable law and circumstances. It is not a blanket permission to copy online content.

Creative Commons

Creative Commons provides licenses that creators can use to communicate how other people may share or adapt their works. Each license has specific conditions.

Responsible Use of Digital Content

  • Check the license before using an image or video.
  • Ask permission when required.
  • Provide proper attribution.
  • Do not claim another person's work as your own.
  • Respect restrictions on copying and distribution.
  • Use public domain or properly licensed resources when appropriate.
Example: Finding a photograph on Google does not automatically mean you can use it in your project. Check its license and permission requirements first.

Responsible AI Use

Artificial Intelligence refers to computer systems designed to perform tasks that usually require human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing patterns, and generating content.

How Students Can Use AI

  • Ask AI to explain difficult lessons in simpler words.
  • Use it to brainstorm possible project ideas.
  • Request grammar and writing suggestions.
  • Practice programming concepts and troubleshoot errors.
  • Create study questions and learning exercises.
  • Organize research ideas before writing.

Risks of Using AI

  • AI may produce incorrect or outdated information.
  • AI may generate fake citations or references.
  • Overdependence can reduce independent thinking.
  • Sharing personal information can create privacy risks.
  • Undisclosed AI use may violate school policies.

Responsible AI Practices

  • Verify AI-generated information with credible sources.
  • Understand and review all generated code.
  • Follow your school's AI guidelines.
  • Disclose AI assistance when required.
  • Do not use AI to cheat during examinations.
  • Protect confidential and personal information.
Remember: AI should support your learning, not replace your own knowledge, creativity, effort, and responsibility.

Professional Ethics

Ethical values that guide students and future IT professionals.

Honesty & Integrity

Provide truthful information, avoid cheating, and demonstrate honesty in academic and professional work.

Responsibility & Accountability

Accept responsibility for your decisions, actions, errors, and contributions to projects.

Respect for Others

Respect people's rights, privacy, opinions, and dignity in online and offline environments.

Intellectual Property

Respect the ideas, software, designs, and creations of others through proper attribution and licensing.

Professional Competence

Continue learning, improve technical skills, and perform tasks carefully and responsibly.

Social Responsibility

Consider how technology affects individuals, communities, the environment, and society.

Responsible AI

Use AI transparently, verify its results, and avoid using technology to deceive or harm others.

Accessibility

Help create technology that different people can understand and use, including people with disabilities.

Privacy & Security

Privacy and security are important in digital environments. Students and IT professionals should understand how personal information is collected, used, stored, and protected.

What Is Data Privacy?

Data privacy concerns the proper handling of personal information and the rights of individuals regarding their data.

What Is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity involves protecting computers, networks, systems, and information against unauthorized access, attacks, and damage.

Common Online Threats

  • Phishing: Fake messages or websites designed to steal information.
  • Malware: Software created to harm or exploit a computer system.
  • Ransomware: Malware that locks or encrypts data and demands payment.
  • Identity theft: Unauthorized use of another person's personal information.
  • Social engineering: Manipulating people into revealing confidential information.

How to Protect Yourself

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • Avoid suspicious links and attachments.
  • Keep your operating system and applications updated.
  • Back up important files regularly.
  • Limit the personal information you share online.

Privacy in the Philippines

The Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) establishes protections for personal information and responsibilities for organizations that process personal data.

Safety reminder: A legitimate organization should not ask you to disclose your password or one-time verification code through a suspicious message.
