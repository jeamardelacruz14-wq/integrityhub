Plagiarism Awareness

Plagiarism is presenting another person's words, ideas, work, or information as your own without proper acknowledgment. It violates academic honesty and the original author's intellectual rights.

Common Types of Plagiarism

  • Copying text directly without quotation marks or citation.
  • Using another person's ideas without giving credit.
  • Submitting someone else's work as your own.
  • Paraphrasing without acknowledging the original source.
  • Reusing previous work without permission when prohibited.

How to Avoid Plagiarism

  • Write using your own understanding.
  • Use quotation marks for directly copied words.
  • Cite the source of borrowed ideas and information.
  • Keep track of websites, books, and materials you use.
  • Review your work before submitting it.

Example: Instead of copying an article paragraph, read and understand the information, write it in your own words, and cite the original author.

Citation & Referencing

Citation is the process of acknowledging the source of information used in your academic work. It gives credit to original authors and allows readers to find the original source.

Why Citations Matter

  • Give proper credit to the original author.
  • Prevent plagiarism.
  • Support your statements with reliable evidence.
  • Allow readers to verify information.

APA 7th Edition Example

Book Reference:

Author, A. A. (Year). Title of book. Publisher.


Website Reference:

Author. (Year, Month Day). Title of page. Website Name. URL

Example of In-text Citation

Technology improves access to information (Santos, 2024).

Citation Tips

  • Always record the author's name.
  • Include the publication date when available.
  • Include the title and URL for online materials.
  • Use the citation format required by your instructor.

Copyright & Fair Use

Copyright protects original creative works such as writing, photos, music, videos, artwork, software, and other intellectual creations. It gives creators rights over how their work is reproduced and used.

Examples of Copyrighted Materials

  • Photographs and illustrations
  • Music and recordings
  • Movies and videos
  • Books and articles
  • Software and digital designs

Responsible Use of Content

  • Ask permission when required.
  • Check the license before using materials.
  • Credit the creator properly.
  • Do not assume everything online is free to use.
  • Use public domain or properly licensed materials when possible.

Remember: Giving credit does not automatically give permission to use copyrighted material. Always check the license and applicable rules.

Responsible AI Use

Artificial Intelligence can help students understand lessons, brainstorm ideas, practice skills, and improve writing. However, AI should be used responsibly and should not replace a student's own thinking and learning.

Responsible Ways to Use AI

  • Ask AI to explain difficult lessons.
  • Use AI for brainstorming and planning.
  • Ask for feedback on grammar and writing.
  • Use AI to practice programming concepts.
  • Verify AI-generated facts using credible sources.

What Students Should Avoid

  • Submitting AI-generated work without following school rules.
  • Using fake or AI-generated references.
  • Trusting AI answers without verification.
  • Using AI to cheat during exams.
  • Sharing private information with AI tools.

Ethical AI Rule: Use AI to assist your learning, not to replace your knowledge, effort, creativity, and responsibility.

Professional Ethics

Ethical principles that guide students and future IT professionals in building responsible and trustworthy technology.

Intellectual Property Rights

Respect the work, creations, code, and ideas of others. Give proper attribution and follow licenses and permissions.

Responsibility & Accountability

Accept responsibility for your work, decisions, errors, and contributions to projects and technology.

Honesty & Integrity

Provide truthful information, avoid cheating, and maintain honesty in academic work and professional activities.

Respect for Others

Respect the privacy, rights, opinions, and dignity of classmates, teachers, users, and other people.

Responsible AI

Understand, verify, and disclose AI assistance. Do not use technology to deceive or mislead.

Accessibility

Design websites and technology that are easy to understand and accessible to different users and abilities.

Privacy & Security

Protecting users' information is an important responsibility when developing websites and digital systems.

Privacy Protection

We avoid collecting unnecessary personal information. This website does not ask users for passwords, financial information, or sensitive personal details.

Cybersecurity Awareness

Users should avoid suspicious links, use strong passwords, protect their accounts, and keep devices updated.

Safe Website Development

Developers should avoid harmful scripts, unauthorized tracking, and insecure handling of user data.

Responsible Data Handling

Information should only be collected when necessary, handled responsibly, and protected from unauthorized access.
